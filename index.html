Engineering Specifications

Zero Dependency Vanilla Architecture

NexusHub is engineered with precision by Devdatta Chaudhuri using strictly vanilla HTML5, modern CSS3, and ES6+ JavaScript. Designed with 3D glassmorphic optics, tactile mouse-tracking perspective transforms, pure Canvas QR generation, and real-time validation without external dependencies.

Pure Canvas QR Generator

Built-in Reed-Solomon error correction and Byte Mode encoding drawn directly onto HTML5 Canvas without third-party scripts.

3D Perspective Physics

Interactive multi-layered depth utilizing CSS preserve-3d, responsive tilt mathematics, and spring easing transitions.

State & Ticket Persistence

Instant seat capacity adjustment, duplicate email prevention, and full offline LocalStorage synchronization.
